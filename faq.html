Builds & the studio

More than you need it to. Every piece is built around the body it’s for and then overbuilt from there, designed to your weight and to how you actually intend to use it, including the loads that come with movement rather than sitting still. If you have a specific requirement, put it in your commission request and I’ll build to it.

Most builds take 5–9 weeks from consultation to delivery, depending on complexity and the current queue.
