How it works

Every piece starts with a conversation

About scenes, about mobility, about what you've imagined and never asked for. The piece is the outcome, the conversation comes first.

1
Ask

Send a commission request. It tells me what you’re imagining, so I can tell you whether I can build it and roughly what it would cost.

2
Reserve

A $200 design consultation holds your spot in my build queue, credited in full toward your piece.

3
Converse

We talk scenes, mobility, materials, and measurements, in a range of positions, for the most comfortable fit.

4
Build

I cut, weld, paint, and upholster your piece by hand, most builds take 5–9 weeks.

5
Deliver

Shipped to you in a plain, nondescript box, with your consultation credited against the final invoice.
